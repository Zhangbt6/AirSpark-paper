AirSpark figure bundle v0.30 — research design, no measured results

Chinese Figure 2 (fig02-rsi-method-zh.svg/png) is updated to v0.30.
It preserves the clockwise six-stage cycle and adds explicit fixed allocation,
configuration/operation choices, append-only records and revisable experience.
Untrained attempts retain the student and accepted configuration; outcomes are
recorded as unmeasured rather than zero learning gain.

Other Chinese figures and all English figures remain the v0.23 assets.
The English method figure is an earlier design, not a translation of v0.30.
English LaTeX synchronization remains deferred by the author.

SVG text and geometry are editable. PNG files are exported at 2x resolution.
To regenerate Chinese Figure 2 only:
  python scripts/build-rsi-figure-v030.py
Then render that SVG using sharp with density 144. Do not rerun earlier figure
builders afterward: they would overwrite this update. Other scripts are
included for provenance and earlier figures.
